90. Surah Al-Balad

The City

20 verses · Revealed in Makkah · Juz 30

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

In the Name of God, the Lord of Mercy, the Giver of Mercy (Abdel Haleem)

✦
90:1·Juz 30·Page 594

﴿١﴾

I swear by this city [i.e., Makkah]

﴿٢﴾

﴿٣﴾
