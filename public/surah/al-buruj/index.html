85. Surah Al-Buruj

The Mansions of the Stars

22 verses · Revealed in Makkah · Juz 30

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

In the Name of God, the Lord of Mercy, the Giver of Mercy (Abdel Haleem)
