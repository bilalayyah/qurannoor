15. Surah Al-Hijr

The Rocky Tract

99 verses · Revealed in Makkah · Juz 14

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

In the Name of God, the Lord of Mercy, the Giver of Mercy (Abdel Haleem)

✦
15:1·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿١﴾

Alif, Lām, Rā. These are the verses of the Book and a clear Qur’ān [i.e., recitation].

15:2·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٢﴾

Perhaps those who disbelieve will wish that they had been Muslims.

15:3·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٣﴾

Let them eat and enjoy themselves and be diverted by [false] hope, for they are going to know.

15:4·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٤﴾

And We did not destroy any city but that for it was a known decree.

15:5·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٥﴾

No nation will precede its term, nor will they remain thereafter.

15:6·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٦﴾

And they say, "O you upon whom the message has been sent down, indeed you are mad.

15:7·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٧﴾

Why do you not bring us the angels, if you should be among the truthful?"

15:8·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٨﴾

We do not send down the angels except with truth; and they [i.e., the disbelievers] would not then be reprieved.

15:9·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿٩﴾

Indeed, it is We who sent down the message [i.e., the Qur’ān], and indeed, We will be its guardian.

15:10·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿١٠﴾

And We had certainly sent [messengers] before you, [O Muḥammad], among the sects of the former peoples.

15:11·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿١١﴾

And no messenger would come to them except that they ridiculed him.

15:12·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿١٢﴾

Thus do We insert it [i.e., denial] into the hearts of the criminals.

15:13·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿١٣﴾

They will not believe in it, while there has already occurred the precedent of the former peoples.

15:14·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿١٤﴾

And [even] if We opened to them a gate from the heaven and they continued therein to ascend,

15:15·Juz 14·Page 262

﴿١٥﴾

They would say, "Our eyes have only been dazzled. Rather, we are a people affected by magic."
