104. Surah Al-Humazah

The Traducer

9 verses · Revealed in Makkah · Juz 30

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

In the Name of God, the Lord of Mercy, the Giver of Mercy (Abdel Haleem)

✦
104:1·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿١﴾

Woe to every scorner and mocker

104:2·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٢﴾

Who collects wealth and [continuously] counts it.

104:3·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٣﴾

He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal.

104:4·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٤﴾

No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher.

104:5·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٥﴾

And what can make you know what is the Crusher?

104:6·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٦﴾

It is the fire of Allāh, [eternally] fueled,

104:7·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٧﴾

Which mounts directed at the hearts.

104:8·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٨﴾

Indeed, it [i.e., Hellfire] will be closed down upon them

104:9·Juz 30·Page 601

﴿٩﴾

In extended columns.
