Accessibility Statement

Our ongoing commitment to making Quran reading and listening comfortable, navigable, and legible for every visitor.

1. Accessibility Measures Implemented

  • Skip-to-Content & Landmarks: Keyboard users can jump directly past navigation to main reading content using the skip link and semantic landmarks.
  • Keyboard Focus Visibility: High-contrast emerald focus outlines are applied across links, buttons, inputs, and reader controls.
  • Reading Contrast & Theme Modes: Visitors can switch between Light, Sepia Parchment, and Dark Forest themes designed for comfortable low-glare reading.
  • Scalable Arabic & Translation Typography: Reader Settings allow direct adjustment of Arabic script sizes (16px–48px, default 28px) and translation sizes (14px–24px, default 17px), alongside Uthmani (Hafs) and IndoPak scripts across three Arabic fonts (Scheherazade New, Noto Naskh Arabic, and Lateef).
  • RTL & Reduced Motion: Strict right-to-left dir="rtl" isolation preserves Arabic verse integrity, and motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

2. Conformance Status

QuranNoor.com is designed following recognized WCAG accessibility best practices, though a formal third-party WCAG 2.1 AA certification audit has not yet been completed. We treat accessibility as an ongoing engineering discipline and welcome user testing feedback.

3. Reporting Accessibility Barriers

If you experience difficulty using a screen reader, keyboard navigation, or audio player controls on any device, please report the issue and your browser/assistive technology details on our Contact page.
