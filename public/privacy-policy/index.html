Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026. QuranNoor is built with a local-first reading experience that does not require user accounts or login profiles.

1. Information We Collect

QuranNoor.com does not currently offer user registration, user accounts, or personal user databases. We do not collect your name, address, payment card information, or reading bookmarks on our servers.

Our Contact Us form is live. When you submit it, the name, email address, subject, and message you voluntarily provide are first sent to QuranNoor's own server-side contact endpoint (/api/contact), which then delivers your message to us by email using FormSubmit as the delivery service, so we can respond to your inquiry. We use the information you submit solely for that purpose and retain it only as long as needed to handle your inquiry; FormSubmit's own handling of the submission is governed by its privacy policy.

2. Automatically Collected Technical Information

When your browser accesses QuranNoor.com, our hosting infrastructure (and upstream API requests routed through our server proxy) processes standard HTTP request metadata such as IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, timestamp, and HTTP status code in transient server or edge network logs for security, rate limiting, and operational diagnostics.

3. Browser Local Storage & Cookies

Instead of requiring an account, QuranNoor saves your reader preferences (selected theme, Arabic script, font size, preferred English translation, preferred reciter), bookmarked Ayahs, and last-read Surah progress exclusively inside your own browser's localStorage. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings. Please see our Cookie Policy for full details.

4. Analytics & Performance Monitoring

QuranNoor.com does not currently run third-party behavioral analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics or session replay trackers). If privacy-respecting performance monitoring or analytics are added later, this Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated before activation.

5. Advertising

QuranNoor.com currently shows no advertisements and loads no advertising scripts. If advertising is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated before any advertising script is activated, and advertising cookies will only operate as described in the Cookie Policy.

A legacy ads.txt file remains published on the site from a previous, now-removed advertising setup. It does not serve or enable any advertising, and no advertising runs on QuranNoor.com.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Quran Foundation Content API & Audio CDN: Quranic Arabic verses, translations, and recitation audio streams are retrieved through our server-side proxy and streamed from verified Quran Foundation media endpoints.
  • FormSubmit: Our live contact form first sends the name, email address, subject, and message you submit to our own server-side contact endpoint, which then delivers your message to us by email using FormSubmit as the delivery service. See section 1 above for what is sent and how it is retained.
  • Aladhan Prayer Times API: When you use the prayer-times feature, your latitude and longitude are sent to Aladhan to calculate prayer times and the Qibla bearing for your location.
  • BigDataCloud and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): When your location is detected for prayer times, your latitude and longitude are sent to BigDataCloud (with Nominatim as a fallback) for reverse geocoding, so we can display a readable place name such as a city and country instead of raw coordinates.
  • IP-based location services (ipapi.co, ipwho.is, and geojs.io): If precise device location is unavailable, the prayer-times feature falls back to estimating your location from your IP address. Each of these services is queried in turn until one responds, and each receives your IP address when it is queried. They return only an approximate city-level location, which is then used to fetch prayer times from Aladhan.
  • Google Fonts: Typography files (Scheherazade New, Noto Naskh Arabic, Lateef, Noto Nastaliq Urdu, Plus Jakarta Sans, and Source Serif 4) are requested by your browser from Google Fonts CDN.
  • Browser Speech Synthesis: Optional spoken English translation playback runs locally inside your device's browser using built-in Web Speech synthesis.

Your detected location — including its latitude and longitude — is also saved in your own browser's local storage (prayer preferences and prayer-times cache) so prayer times can be shown again without re-detecting your location. The cached prayer-times entry is keyed to your coordinates rounded to three decimal places (about 100 m). Clearing your browser's site data removes this stored location.

7. Data Retention, Security & Privacy Rights

Because reader preferences and bookmarks reside solely on your device, you hold direct control over retention and deletion. Where contact submissions or server logs are processed, data is retained only as long as operationally necessary. Depending on your applicable laws (such as GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may request access, correction, or deletion of any personal correspondence via our Contact page.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy whenever new technical integrations (such as analytics or email delivery) are enabled. Any revision will carry an updated effective date at the top of this page.

Advertising & Third-Party Cookies

QuranNoor.com currently shows no advertisements and loads no advertising scripts, so no advertising cookies are placed by this website. If advertising is introduced in the future, this section will be updated before any advertising script is activated.
